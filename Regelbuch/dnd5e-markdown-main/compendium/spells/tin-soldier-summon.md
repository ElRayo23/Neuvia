# Tin Soldier Summon
*3nd-level, Abjuration*  

- **Casting time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M
- **Duration:** 1 Minute

Summoning. As an action, you summon a Tin Soldier in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you. The soldier remains for 1 minute, until reduced to 0 hit points, or until you dismiss it (no action required).
- The soldier acts immediately after your turn.
- It follows your verbal commands.
- You can command it using a bonus action.
- You can have only one Tin Soldier summoned at a time.
- Once used, this ability cannot be used again until you finish a long rest.
Tin Soldier
Small construct
Stat	Value
AC	15
HP	20
Speed	30 ft.
STR	14 (+2)
DEX	12 (+1)
CON	14 (+2)
INT	4 (-3)
WIS	10 (+0)
CHA	3 (-4)

Bayonet. Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 1d8 + 2 piercing damage.

Protective Formation (PF). While the Tin Soldier is within 5 feet of you, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

Last Stand. When the Tin Soldier is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes. Each creature within 5 feet must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw (+1 for the caster due to PF), taking 2d6 piercing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
