# Alma

> Quelle: [Originalnotiz in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/1ae60f7443dc809f851eea8236f29c55)

### <b>Alma – Redbrand Striker (CR 1)</b>

<b>Medium Humanoid (Half-Elf), Chaotic Neutral</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 16 (Unarmored Defense – DEX + WIS)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 22 (5d8+5)

<b>Speed:</b> 40 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 12 (+1)
- <b>DEX:</b> 18 (+4)
- <b>CON:</b> 12 (+1)
- <b>INT:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>WIS:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>CHA:</b> 10 (+0)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

DEX +6, WIS +4

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Evasion:</b> If Alma makes a DEX save against an effect that deals half damage on a success, she takes <b>no damage</b> on a success and <b>half damage</b> on a failure.
- <b>Step of the Shadow:</b> Alma can take the <b>Dash</b>, <b>Disengage</b>, or <b>Hide</b> action as a <b>bonus action</b>.
- <b>Flurry of Blows (Recharge 5-6):</b> After hitting with an unarmed strike, Alma can make <b>two additional</b> unarmed strikes.

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Multiattack:</b> Alma makes <b>two attacks</b> (Unarmed or Shortsword).
- <b>Unarmed Strike:</b> +6 to hit, 1d6+4 bludgeoning.
- <b>Shortsword:</b> +6 to hit, 1d6+4 piercing.
- <b>Sweeping Kick (1/Day):</b> Alma sweeps low, forcing all creatures within 5 feet to make a <b>DC 14 Dexterity</b> save or be <b>knocked prone</b>.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Quick Step:</b> Alma moves <b>10 feet</b> without provoking opportunity attacks.
- <b>Focused Strike (1/Short Rest):</b> Alma makes an unarmed strike with <b>advantage</b>
