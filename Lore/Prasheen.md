# Prasheen

> Quelle: [Originalnotiz in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/1ae60f7443dc8051b350e8e405f388f0)

### <b>Prasheen – Redbrand Toxomancer (CR 1)</b>

<b>Medium Humanoid (Half-Orc), Chaotic Evil</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 13 (Mage Armor)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 27 (6d6)

<b>Speed:</b> 30 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>DEX:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>CON:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>INT:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>WIS:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>CHA:</b> 12 (+1)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

CON +4, INT +4

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Darkvision (60 ft.):</b> Can see in darkness as if it were dim light.
- <b>Poison Resistance:</b> Prasheen has <b>resistance</b> to poison damage.
- <b>Toxic Touch:</b> Any creature that hits Prasheen with a melee attack takes 1d4 poison damage.

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Spellcasting:</b> Prasheen is a 3rd-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following spells prepared:
  - <b>Cantrips (at will):</b>
    - <b>Poison Spray:</b> <i>Instantaneous</i>. You release a puff of poison. One creature within 10 feet must make a <b>Constitution saving throw</b> or take <b>1d12 poison damage</b>.
    - <b>Prestidigitation:</b> <i>1 action</i>. Perform minor magical tricks, such as cleaning objects, creating small sensory effects, or altering colors.
    - <b>Thaumaturgy:</b> <i>1 action</i>. You can create minor wonders, such as altering your voice, causing flames to flicker, or creating instantaneous sounds.
  - <b>1st Level (4 slots):</b>
    - <b>Disguise Self:</b> <i>1 action</i>. You change your appearance for up to 1 hour, making yourself appear as someone else.
    - <b>Mage Armor:</b> <i>1 action</i>. You touch a willing creature to surround it with a magical force, granting a <b>+3 AC</b> for 8 hours.
    - <b>Ray of Sickness:</b> <i>1 action</i>. A ray of sickening green energy strikes a creature within 60 feet. The target must make a <b>Constitution saving throw</b> or take <b>2d8 poison damage</b> and become <b>poisoned</b> for 1 minute.
  - <b>2nd Level (2 slots):</b>
    - <b>Misty Step:</b> <i>Bonus action</i>. You teleport up to <b>30 feet</b> to an unoccupied space you can see.
    - <b>Gaseous Form:</b> <i>1 action</i>. You transform yourself or a willing creature into a misty, vaporous form for 1 hour. The target can move through small openings and has resistance to all damage except force and psychic. The target can’t attack or cast spells in this form.
- <b>Toxic Cloud (Recharge 5-6):</b> Prasheen releases a cloud of poisonous gas in a 10-foot radius. All creatures within range must make a <b>DC 12 Constitution saving throw</b> or take <b>2d6 poison damage</b> and become <b>poisoned</b> for 1 minute. Creatures that succeed take half damage and aren't poisoned.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Mist Step (Recharge 5-6):</b> As a bonus action, Prasheen can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see, leaving behind a faint, toxic mist that lasts for 1 round.
