# Annok

> Quelle: [Originalnotiz in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/1ae60f7443dc809f9818d293b6036c73)

### <b>Annok – Redbrand Tinkerer (CR 1)</b>

<b>Small Humanoid (Halfling), Chaotic Neutral</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 12 (Leather Armor)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 20 (5d6+5)

<b>Speed:</b> 25 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 8 (-1)
- <b>DEX:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>CON:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>INT:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>WIS:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>CHA:</b> 12 (+1)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

DEX +5, INT +5

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Halfling Nimbleness:</b> Annok can move through the space of any creature that is <b>larger</b> than him.
- <b>Lucky:</b> When Annok rolls a 1 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, he can reroll it and must use the new roll.
- <b>Explosive Expertise:</b> Annok is proficient with alchemical and explosive devices, and his <b>bombs</b> deal <b>1d6 fire damage</b> in a 5-foot radius on a failed <b>DC 12 Dexterity</b> save (half damage on a success).
- <b>Tinkerer's Insight:</b> Annok can use his action to craft small explosive devices or traps (such as <b>Thunderstones</b> or <b>Alchemist's Fire</b>) during a short rest.

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Multiattack:</b> Annok makes two attacks with his <b>pistol</b> or <b>explosive device</b>.
- <b>Pistol (Improvised Weapon):</b> +5 to hit, 1d4+3 piercing.
- <b>Explosive Device (3/day):</b> Annok throws a small explosive, causing a <b>5-foot radius explosion</b>. Creatures in the area must make a <b>DC 12 Dexterity</b> save or take <b>1d6 fire damage</b> (half on a success).
- <b>Smoke Bomb (1/Day):</b> Annok throws a <b>smoke bomb</b> at a point within 20 feet. A 10-foot-radius cloud of thick smoke surrounds that point for 1 minute, heavily obscuring the area. Creatures within the cloud have <b>disadvantage</b> on attacks.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Quick Throw (3/day):</b> Annok can throw an explosive device or alchemical item as a <b>bonus action</b>.
