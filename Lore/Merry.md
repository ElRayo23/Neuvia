# Merry

> Quelle: [Originalnotiz in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/1ae60f7443dc809c88bcf61bf4be5094)

### <b>Marry – Redbrand Enforcer (CR 1)</b>

<b>Small Humanoid (Dwarf), Chaotic Evil</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 14 (Chain Shirt)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 29 (5d8+10)

<b>Speed:</b> 25 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 15 (+2)
- <b>DEX:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>CON:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>INT:</b> 9 (-1)
- <b>WIS:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>CHA:</b> 11 (+0)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

CON +5, STR +4

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Dwarven Resilience:</b> Advantage on saving throws against poison; resistance to poison damage.
- <b>Stubborn as a Rock:</b> Marry has advantage on checks against being <b>grappled, shoved, or knocked prone</b>.
- <b>Dirty Fighter:</b> If Marry hits a creature that is <b>prone or grappled</b>, she deals an additional <b>1d6</b> damage.

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Mace:</b> +4 to hit, 1d6+2 bludgeoning.
- <b>Headbutt:</b> +4 to hit, 1d4+2 bludgeoning. If the target is Medium or smaller, they must succeed on a <b>DC 12 Strength</b> save or be <b>pushed 5 feet</b>.
- <b>Gut Punch (Recharge 5-6):</b> Marry punches a creature in the stomach. The target must make a <b>DC 12 Constitution</b> save or be <b>stunned</b> until the start of Marry’s next turn.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Drink Up!</b> As a bonus action, Marry drinks from her hidden flask, gaining <b>5 temporary hit points</b>.
