### <b>Shan – Redbrand Swordsman (CR 1)</b>

<b>Medium Humanoid (Tiefling), Chaotic Neutral</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 13 (Studded Leather Armor)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 22 (5d8+5)

<b>Speed:</b> 30 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>DEX:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>CON:</b> 12 (+1)
- <b>INT:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>WIS:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>CHA:</b> 13 (+1)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

DEX +5, WIS +4

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Infernal Legacy:</b> Shan knows the <i>Thaumaturgy</i> cantrip. He can cast <b>Hellish Rebuke</b> (2/Day) at 2nd level as a reaction.
- <b>Flame Resistance:</b> Shan has <b>resistance</b> to fire damage.

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Katana:</b> +5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing (or 1d10+3 if wielded two-handed).
- <b>Precise Strike (1/Short Rest):</b> Shan can spend a bonus action to gain <b>advantage</b> on his next attack roll within the next minute.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Step of the Wind:</b> Shan can take the <b>Disengage</b> or <b>Dash</b> action as a bonus action.
- <b>Quick Draw (3/day):</b> Shan can draw and attack with his katana as a bonus action.
