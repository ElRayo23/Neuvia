### <b>Velik – (CR 2)</b>

<b>Medium Humanoid (Human), Chaotic Evil</b>

<b>Armor Class:</b> 15 (Breastplate)

<b>Hit Points:</b> 40

<b>Speed:</b> 30 ft.

### <b>Attributes:</b>

- <b>STR:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>DEX:</b> 14 (+2)
- <b>CON:</b> 16 (+3)
- <b>INT:</b> 8 (-1)
- <b>WIS:</b> 10 (+0)
- <b>CHA:</b> 9 (-1)

### <b>Saving Throws:</b>

STR +5, CON +5

### <b>Abilities:</b>

- <b>Tough as Nails:</b> If Velik takes 10 damage or less from a single attack, he reduces it by 3 (damage resistance against weak hits).
- <b>Intimidating Presence:</b> He gains advantage on Intimidation checks against creatures of CR 1 or lower (mostly thugs and commoners).

### <b>Actions:</b>

- <b>Longsword:</b> +5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing (1d10+3 if wielded two-handed).
- <b>Dagger:</b> +5 to hit, 1d4+3 piercing (melee or ranged 20/60 ft.).
- <b>Reckless Swing (2/Day):</b> Velik makes a melee attack with advantage, but attacks against him have advantage until his next turn.

### <b>Bonus Actions:</b>

- <b>Berserker Swing (3/day):</b> If Velik hits a target with a melee weapon attack, he can immediately make one additional attack against the same target.
- <b>Shoulder Bash:</b> Velik shoves a creature within 5 feet. The target must succeed on a <b>DC 13 Strength</b> saving throw or be pushed 5 feet and knocked <b>prone</b>.
