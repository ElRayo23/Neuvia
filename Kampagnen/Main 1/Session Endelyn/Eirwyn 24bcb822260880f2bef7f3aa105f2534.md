# Eirwyn

**Background:** Free Spirit

**Alignment:** Neutral Good

**HP:** 38 (5 × 8 + CON bonus)

**AC:** 16 (Leather armor + shield)

**Speed:** 30 ft

**Proficiency Bonus:** +3

INT 8 (-1) |

## **Spells**

### **Cantrips**

**Produce Flame** – Conjuration. A flickering flame appears in your hand. Lasts for the duration. You can attack with it (1d8 fire) or use it as light.

**Guidance** – Divination. Touch a willing creature; it adds 1d4 to one ability check before the spell ends.

**Thorn Whip** – Conjuration. Create a vine whip; melee spell attack, 1d6 piercing, pull target 10 ft closer.

---

### **Level 1 Spells (4 slots)**

**Entangle** – 20-ft square of grasping weeds; STR save or restrained; area is difficult terrain.

**Faerie Fire** – 20-ft cube; creatures in area outline in light; attacks against them have advantage; invisibility negated.

**Cure Wounds** – Touch; heal 1d8 + WIS modifier.

**Goodberry** – Create up to 10 berries; each heals 1 HP and nourishes for a day.

**Detect Magic** – Concentration up to 10 min; sense magic within 30 ft.

**Detect Poison and Disease** – Sense poisons, poisonous creatures, and diseases within 30 ft.

---

### **Level 2 Spells (3 slots)**

**Moonbeam** – 5-ft radius, 40-ft high cylinder; Con save or 2d10 radiant; shapechangers save at disadvantage.

**Flaming Sphere** – 5-ft diameter sphere of fire; rams creatures for 2d6 fire; moves as bonus action.

**Lesser Restoration** – Touch; cure one disease or condition (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned).

**Barkskin** – Touch; target’s AC becomes 16 if not higher.

**Gust of Wind** – 60-ft line of strong wind; pushes creatures 15 ft; disperses gas/fog; extinguishes flames. STR save

---

### **Level 3 Spells (2 slots)**

**Call Lightning** – Storm cloud forms; 3d10 lightning in 60-ft radius (Dex save half) each round.

**Plant Growth** – Either enrich plants in 100-ft radius, or make 100-ft radius of plants into difficult terrain (costs 4 ft per 1 ft movement).

**Dispel Magic** – End spell effects on a creature, object, or area; auto-ends if 3rd level or lower, otherwise check.

**Water Breathing** – Up to 10 willing creatures can breathe underwater for 24 hours.
