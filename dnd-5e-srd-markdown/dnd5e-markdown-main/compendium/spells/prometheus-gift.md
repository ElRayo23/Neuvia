# Prometheus' Gift
2nd-level Divination

Casting Time: 1 Bonus Action
Range: Self
Components: V, S
Duration: Instantaneous

You call upon Prometheus, offering a fragment of your life force in exchange for forbidden knowledge.

You take 2d6 necrotic damage, which cannot be reduced or prevented.

In return, Prometheus whispers ancient magical knowledge directly into your mind.

Choose one option:

1. Knowledge: You may cast one spell (of the same level as this) of any list (cleric, bard, etc). You can only use this once per Long Rest.
2. Power: You gain 2 temporary Sorcery Points until your next Long Rest. You cannot exceed your maximum points.
